PARLY — DUSK

Two suns meeting
at the edge of the day.

The phone is the table. Each speaker is a sun at one of its edges.

Warm bloom — apricot, peach, terracotta. The user's side.

Cool bloom — periwinkle, seafoam, iris. The partner's side.

The seam at the centre is where their lights meet. Not a divider — an encounter. The translation stays pure white; colour lives in the atmosphere, never in the words.

The disc is no longer a polished puck — it is a watercolour stain that breathes with your voice. Asymmetric, never a laser ring.
